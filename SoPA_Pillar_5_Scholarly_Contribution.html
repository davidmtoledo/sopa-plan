Strategic Plan 2025–2035  |  Pillar 5

Scholarly Contribution

Position SoPA at the forefront of the performing arts conversation through faculty publication, performance, composition, and faith-integrated inquiry.

Pillar 5 Goals & Objectives
Full Goal Breakdown
Scholarly Contribution is the Collinsworth School's commitment to a faculty culture that produces original, recognized work across the full range of scholarly and artistic expression. SoPA expects and supports faculty contribution through peer-reviewed publication, original composition, professional performance, and faith-integrated inquiry. By 2035, the Collinsworth School of Performing Arts will be at the forefront of the global conversation regarding the purpose and function of the performing arts in culture.
Goals at a Glance
5.1Expand Diverse Faculty Scholarly Contributions
5.2Advance Artistic Inquiry and Creative Contribution
5.3Lead Faith-Integrated and Interdisciplinary Scholarship
5.1 Expand Diverse Faculty Scholarly Contributions

Increase the volume and visibility of faculty scholarship across all four Boyer domains — Discovery, Integration, Application, and Teaching — with particular emphasis on growing peer-reviewed publication and conference presence as the school's academic profile matures.

  • Maintain an annual faculty research agenda requirement tracking output and growth across all four Boyer domains
  • Support scholarly productivity through strategic course assignment, prioritizing repeat course assignments for faculty with active research agendas to reduce preparation burden and protect creative and scholarly time
  • Develop an internal travel funding policy and budget to support conference presentations and professional performances
  • Build a publicly accessible faculty scholarship portfolio on the school's website
  • Recognize and celebrate faculty scholarly achievement through annual awards and school communications
  • Measurable target: 100% of full-time faculty maintain an active research agenda with documented year-over-year growth across at least one Boyer domain
5.2 Advance Artistic Inquiry and Creative Contribution

Establish the Collinsworth School as a recognized site of significant creative output through professional performances, original compositions, new works, and recordings at the national and international level.

  • Establish expectations and tracking for faculty professional performances at regional, national, and international venues
  • Build a culture of original composition and new work development across Music and Theatre Arts faculty
  • Pursue recording projects and produced albums that document and distribute faculty creative work beyond the performance moment
  • Connect faculty artistic output to the school's national visibility strategy — ensuring performances and premieres generate press, reputation, and recruitment pipeline
  • Develop a SoPA record label as a long-term vehicle for faculty and student creative output, institutional brand, and revenue generation (target: launched by Year 10)
5.3 Lead Faith-Integrated and Interdisciplinary Scholarship

Develop a body of scholarly work that is distinctively Christian and interdisciplinary, connecting performing arts with theology, cultural studies, and faith formation.

  • Actively connect SoPA faculty to CBU's existing faith integration infrastructure, including TLC fellowships, the Journal of Faith in the Academic Profession, and the Faith Integration in the Academy Conference
  • Develop performing arts-specific faith integration scholarship expectations within the annual research agenda framework
  • Pursue publications and presentations that explicitly advance the national conversation about Christian faith and performing arts practice
  • Develop and publish a faculty-authored edited volume on faith formation across the performing arts disciplines, with chapters representing each division of SoPA (target: published by Year 5)
  • Position the CCCU Arts Deans Leadership Summit as a convening point for faith-integrated performing arts scholarship across Christian higher education
  • Target: SoPA faculty presenting faith-integrated scholarship at national conferences annually by Year 3
Years 1–3: Foundation
  • Annual faculty research agenda requirement implemented across all full-time faculty
  • Internal travel funding policy and budget established
  • Faculty scholarship portfolio live on school website
  • SoPA faculty presenting faith-integrated scholarship at national conferences
  • CCCU Arts Deans Summit and Worship West established as annual convenings
Years 4–7: Expansion
  • Faculty-authored edited volume on faith and performing arts published by Year 5
  • Faculty consistently presenting and performing at national and international venues
  • Recording projects and produced works documenting and distributing faculty creative output
  • CCCU Arts Deans Summit recognized as the premier convening for faith-integrated performing arts scholarship
Years 8–10: Preeminence
  • SoPA record label launched as a vehicle for faculty and student creative output
  • 100% of full-time faculty maintaining active research agendas with documented growth
  • SoPA recognized nationally for leadership in faith-integrated performing arts inquiry
