Strategic Plan

Executive Summary

The Collinsworth School of Performing Arts enters the next decade at a defining moment — for CBU, for Christian higher education, and for the performing arts industries our students will shape. From a baseline of 284 majors in Fall 2025, SoPA carries the momentum of significant recent program development, a growing national profile, and a deepening sense of mission. This plan charts a clear direction for 2025–2035 by centering the people, programs, and priorities that define SoPA's distinctive identity.

Mission

To inspire students to the highest standards of craft and character, preparing them to impact the world through careers in the performing arts.

Vision

A spiritually vibrant community where top-tier artistic and academic preparation converges with intentional personal and professional development.

Strategic Goal — 2035

In 2035, the Collinsworth School of Performing Arts is the premier comprehensive performing arts training unit in Christian higher education and is at the forefront of the global conversation regarding the purpose and function of the performing arts in culture.

The Six Pillars

The plan is organized around six interdependent pillars. Each pillar includes a statement, a full breakdown of goals and objectives, measurable targets, and a ten-year milestone horizon. Click any pillar to expand.

Enrollment Growth establishes the strategic foundation for growing the Collinsworth School from 284 majors in 2025 to 500 by 2035 — the threshold at which SoPA can sustain a comprehensive faculty, justify facility investment, and fund the full range of programming that defines a premier performing arts school. Growth is pursued through program expansion, deliberate recruitment, and retention infrastructure designed to bring new student populations into SoPA while ensuring current students persist to graduation.

Excellence is the relentless pursuit of quality in everything SoPA does: what we teach, how students develop as artists, and whether that preparation translates into meaningful careers. It is measured across three dimensions — Academics, Performance, and Career — that together answer whether SoPA is fulfilling its mission to prepare students for professional impact in the performing arts. Excellence is not a static achievement but a discipline of continuous evaluation, development, and improvement.

Transformative Culture is the Collinsworth School's commitment to intentional formation, shaping not only what students can do, but who they are becoming. SoPA believes that performing artists are shaped not only by technical training but by the full integration of spiritual depth, personal character, professional discipline, and artistic craft. That formation does not happen by accident. It requires intentional design, consistent investment, and a culture where every member of the community — faculty, staff, and students alike — is actively growing.
