Strategic Plan 2025–2035  |  Pillar 6

Missional Infrastructure

Build the infrastructure of leadership, donor base, facilities, and faculty necessary for long-term success.

Pillar 6 Goals & Objectives
Full Goal Breakdown
Preeminence requires more than vision. It requires the organizational depth, financial strength, physical environment, and human capital to sustain it. Missional Infrastructure builds the foundational capacity of the Collinsworth School by developing leaders at every level, cultivating a philanthropic culture that funds ambitious goals, investing in facilities worthy of a premier performing arts program, and hiring the faculty and staff needed to serve a growing, diversifying student body.

6.1 Form High-Capacity Leaders across the Organization

Develop the personnel within SoPA's organizational structure into high-capacity leaders who can carry the school's mission forward, manage expanding responsibilities, and serve as the next generation of institutional leadership.

  • Establish a formal leadership development framework for Division Directors and Area Directors, including mentoring, stretch assignments, and professional growth planning
  • Create intentional succession awareness for key administrative and director roles
  • Build a culture where administrative responsibility is distributed, embraced, and rewarded
  • Year 2 target: every Division Director has a documented professional development plan reviewed annually with the Dean or Associate Dean
  • Year 5 target: at least three Area Directors have taken on expanded responsibilities beyond their defined role, demonstrating pipeline depth
  • Year 10 target: SoPA can demonstrate internal promotion or advancement for at least two leadership roles
6.2 Establish a Sustainable Philanthropic Infrastructure

Build the systems, relationships, and giving culture necessary to grow SoPA's annual donor community from fewer than 20 to 100+ active givers, establish a Friends of SoPA program, and create the pipeline infrastructure for major and planned gifts.

  • Partner with Institutional Advancement to develop a SoPA-specific donor cultivation and stewardship strategy
  • Launch and sustain a Friends of SoPA giving community with tiered membership and meaningful engagement touchpoints
  • Equip the Dean with a structured major gifts strategy, prospect pipeline, and calendar-driven cultivation plan
  • Establish a Dean's Chief of Staff role to provide operational support to the Dean and Associate Deans, enabling senior leadership to focus on donor cultivation and strategic execution (target: role defined, funded, and filled by Year 1)
  • Year 3 target: active donor community reaches 40+; Friends of SoPA formally launched with inaugural giving event
  • Year 7 target: active donor community reaches 75+; at least 2 major gifts ($25K+) secured for programmatic or facility purposes
  • Year 10 target: 100+ active annual donors; endowed scholarship fund established; major gifts pipeline includes at least 5 qualified prospects
6.3 Invest in Facility Expansion that Supports Excellence and Growth

Advance three distinct facility priorities that together create a physical environment commensurate with SoPA's aspirations.

  • Track A — Commercial Music, Rehearsal, and Production Complex: Complete build-out of multi-division rehearsal, studio, and production facility serving Commercial Music, Theatre Arts, and Worship Arts (target: complete and fully operational by Year 1)
  • Track B — Wallace Theatre Renovation: Commission a comprehensive condition and feasibility study; execute highest-impact improvements to extend the venue's life and elevate audience and student experience (target: scope determined by Year 3; renovation complete by Year 5)
  • Track C — Outdoor Performance Venue: Complete architectural feasibility study in partnership with CBU facilities planning; secure institutional alignment; identify lead gift prospects; execute capital campaign and complete construction (target: complete by Year 10)
6.4 Expand Faculty and Staff Capacity to Support Growth

Hire and retain the faculty and staff needed to deliver on SoPA's academic and programmatic commitments, with hiring decisions driven by enrollment growth, new degree programs, and accreditation requirements.

  • Complete confirmed Fall 2026 hires: Associate Director of Choral Studies/Choral Music Education and full-time staff accompanist
  • Establish a hiring philosophy and decision framework that ties future positions to enrollment thresholds and programmatic triggers
  • Review faculty-to-student ratios against NASM standards and peer benchmarks at Year 3; identify and prioritize any critical gaps
  • Year 10 target: SoPA faculty profile reflects a premier program with appropriate terminal degree attainment, active professional currency, and instructional depth across all divisions
Years 1–3: Foundation
  • Commercial Music complex build-out complete and operational
  • Both FA26 faculty and staff hires completed
  • Chief of Staff role defined and filled
  • Friends of SoPA launched; donor tracking system in place
  • Wallace Theatre feasibility study commissioned
  • Division Director development plans implemented
Years 4–7: Expansion
  • Active donors reach 75+; 2 major gifts ($25K+) secured
  • Wallace Theatre renovation complete
  • Outdoor venue feasibility study complete; campaign launched with lead gift secured
  • Three Area Directors demonstrating expanded leadership capacity
Years 8–10: Preeminence
  • Outdoor performance venue complete
  • 100+ active annual donors; endowed scholarship established
  • Internal leadership advancement demonstrated in at least two roles
  • Facility portfolio reflects a premier performing arts institution
